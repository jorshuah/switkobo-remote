Low glare for evening use. Sage identifies the main action; status always has words as well as color. Adapt with semantic light-mode colors.

Readable, native typography

Living room TV

SF Pro with Dynamic Type, 17pt body and 28pt titles. System font fallback shown in this browser board. SF Symbols for controls.

One remote, focused tools

Device and connection status above a large navigation surface. Volume and playback below it. Keyboard and additional controls open native sheets. Settings use a full page.

Interaction and spacing

At least 48pt controls, 12pt gaps, 24pt screen margins. Buttons are always available as an alternative to swipes. Haptics are optional. Draft text survives sheet dismissal; no commands are queued while disconnected.
