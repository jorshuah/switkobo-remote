# App logo sources

YouTube, Netflix and Prime Video SVGs: Simple Icons 11.15.0, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons (CC0; see LICENSE.md).

Disney+: https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons/blob/main/svg/disney-plus.svg

Brand marks belong to their respective owners and identify the TV applications; no affiliation is implied.

Prime Video and Disney+ viewboxes are cropped to the marks; the Disney+ background tile was removed for theme-aware rendering.

YouTube and Netflix were replaced with full wordmarks from https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos/blob/main/logos/youtube.svg and https://github.com/gilbarbara/logos/blob/main/logos/netflix.svg. YouTube has a white-letter variant for dark backgrounds. Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners.
